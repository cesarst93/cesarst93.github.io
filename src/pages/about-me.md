During my training as a **Systems and Computer Engineer**, I became fascinated by **software development**. I built applications for a range of platforms (**desktop, web, mobile and cloud**) using Windows Forms, Windows Presentation Foundation (WPF), ASP, ASP.NET Web Forms, ASP.NET MVC, Windows Phone, Android, Windows Communication Foundation (WCF), ASP.NET Web API, Microsoft Azure and other technologies. As an active member of **Comunidades Microsoft Perú**, I took part in individual and team competitions, earning awards and recognition.

Shortly after landing a job as a programmer analyst, I decided to join the Peruvian Navy, where I was commissioned as a Lieutenant. It has been a career of service to my country that has strengthened my leadership, discipline and teamwork. I had the privilege and the responsibility of leading software development teams at a relatively young age, which sharpened my **project management** skills. I have worked on a range of **web development** and **data analysis** projects, as well as on IT process improvement, cost optimization, budget management, public procurement and digital transformation.

I also hold a second professional specialization in **Electronics Engineering**, with training in embedded systems, control and automation, digital signal processing and telecommunications. I am a volunteer member of the **IEEE Peru Section** and the **PMI Lima Peru Chapter**, where I contribute to activities that promote technology and project management best practices.

A keen follower of technology conferences, workshops and courses, I regularly attend events across many fields of computing and electronics. I am convinced that **artificial intelligence**, **data science** and **cybersecurity** are the new pillars of computing and industry.
